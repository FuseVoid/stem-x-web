1. Scope and Modular Legal Structure

The Root Portal acts as a gateway and directory for FUSE VOID STUDIO products.

  • Root Portal: Does not collect user accounts, execute financial transactions, or process application-specific data.

2. Information We Handle on the Root Portal

We adhere to a strict Privacy-First, Minimal Data philosophy across our infrastructure.

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide
If you contact us directly via support@fusevoid.com, we process your email address and message contents solely to respond to your inquiry. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your contact information.

B. Automated Web Infrastructure Data
When you visit the Root Portal, basic server logs (e.g., IP address, browser type, timestamp) may be briefly processed by our web infrastructure providers (such as Cloudflare) exclusively for security mitigation, DDoS protection, and maintaining network stability.

3. Payments and Third-Party Services

No payment processing occurs on the Root Portal.

Where applicable within our sub-applications, payments and subscriptions are processed by authorized Merchants of Record (such as Lemon Squeezy) or native platform stores (such as the Apple App Store). Each entity processes payment data under its own independent privacy documentation referenced inside those respective hubs.

4. Analytics and Tracking

The Root Portal does not utilize third-party cross-site marketing trackers, behavioral profiling pixels, or advertising cookies.

5. Your Rights (GDPR, CCPA & KVKK Compliance)

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data under global regulations (including the EU General Data Protection Regulation, the California Consumer Privacy Act, and the Turkish Law on Protection of Personal Data No. 6698 / KVKK).

Because the Root Portal does not create user profiles or track visitor identity across the web, we generally do not maintain persistent personal records for Root Portal visitors. For inquiries regarding your privacy rights, contact: support@fusevoid.com.
