Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 19, 2026

Fuse Void MK1 is an offline-first drum machine for iOS. Your patterns, samples, and settings stay on your device — not on our servers.

Summary

We do not collect personal data. We do not use analytics, advertising trackers, or cookies. We do not sell data. The app works without an account and without a constant internet connection.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. Fuse Void does not require sign-in, profile creation, or cloud sync.

We do not receive your name, email, location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or usage analytics through the app.

Data stored on your device

The following stays on your iPad or iPhone unless you export it:

  • Patterns, sequences, and slot data you create
  • Mixer, FX, and performance settings
  • MIDI files you export from the app

Deleting the app removes local app data from your device, subject to iOS behavior.

No tracking

Fuse Void does not use third-party analytics, crash SDKs, ad networks, or social tracking pixels. This website does not use cookies for tracking or profiling.

Website hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The host may process basic server logs (IP, browser type) for security and delivery. We do not use those logs to identify or profile visitors.

App Store purchases

Payments are handled by Apple. We do not receive your card details. See Apple Privacy Policy.

Children

Fuse Void is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Policy updates

We may update this page when the app or legal requirements change. The date above will be revised. Continued use means you accept the updated policy.
